RECOMMENDATION: Approve QuickBooks Online Advanced Payroll subscription to streamline accounting, payroll automation, and reduce manual administrative work.

What: QuickBooks Online Advanced Payroll platform to replace desktop QuickBooks

Cost: $474/month ($4,600/year) after 90-day trial

Payback: Savings offset cost within first 6 months

Timeline: 5-10 business days onboarding with direct QuickBooks support

✨ Key Features & Benefits

✓ Automatic Payroll & IRS Filings
941 and Schedule B payments scheduled automatically each quarter. No manual calculations needed.
✓ Direct Bank Connections
Credit card and bank reconciliation happens automatically. Eliminates 4-5 hours of monthly reconciliation work.
✓ Human Quality Audits
Real person reviews payroll before processing. Catches errors before they become problems.
✓ AI Automation & Categorization
Transactions auto-categorized, reducing data entry and errors. Supports "one-person office" goal.
✓ Advanced Budgeting
Tools needed for municipal-level financial forecasting and analysis.
✓ Seamless Migration
Direct QuickBooks support team onboards from desktop to online (5-10 business days).
