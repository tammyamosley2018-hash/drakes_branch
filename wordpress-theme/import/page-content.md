### A brief history

The town of Drakes Branch is located in Charlotte County, Virginia, about
eighty miles from Richmond and about sixty miles from Danville. Surrounded on
all sides by hills, Drakes Branch is 375 feet above sea level. In 1842 the
Southern Railroad built the Richmond–Danville division passing through the site
of the present town. The town was incorporated in 1903.

There are two streams within the town limits: Twitty's Creek, a tributary of
the Roanoke River, and a branch which gave the town its name and beginnings. A
family by the name of Drakes lived on the heights west of the railroad. Pipe
was laid and water from the headwaters of a little stream was conveyed by
gravity into railroad tanks. The watering spot was named "Drake's Branch."
Later a station was built near where "the branch" flowed into Twitty's Creek,
and a plank road was built to Charlotte Court House, five miles away.

## Pay My Bill

**Page title:** Pay my bill
**Template:** Default page
**Excerpt:**

> Water and sewer bills are due by the 20th of each month.

**Body:**

Bills for water and sewer service are mailed monthly and are due by the **20th
of the month**.

### Ways to pay

**Online** — [Pay your bill online](https://drakesbranch-revmgt.secure.openrda.net/portal/).
Available at any time. Card payments are charged a convenience fee.

> **When you pay online, write in the comments box whether you are paying taxes
> or your water and sewer bill.** Without it we cannot always tell which bill
> the payment is for.

**In person** — cash or card at the Town Office, Monday to Friday between
8:30am and 2:30pm. Card payments are charged a convenience fee.

**By cheque or money order** — drop off at the Town Office at any time. Outside
office hours, use the drop box by the door. **Cheques and money orders only in
the drop box — never cash.**

> **Note:** the original said the same things twice, once before the button and
> once after, both in bold with multiple exclamation marks. I have said it once,
> in a callout, where someone will actually read it before they pay.
>
> I have also separated the payment methods into headed blocks. The original ran
> them together in one paragraph, so "after hours you can place check and money
> order payments only in the drop box" was easy to miss — and that is the
> sentence that stops someone posting cash through a door.
